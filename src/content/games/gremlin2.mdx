# Rare Collection 1

![Cartridge](../images/games/rare1.png)

## Our Involvement

Using binary modification techniques Byteswap Labs created new builds Loaded, Re-Loaded, Hogs of War, and Buggy for this Evercade cartridge.

## Overview

Gremlin returns to Evercade with the explosive action of Loaded and Re-Loaded, the comedic mayhem of Hogs of War and the adventure racing of Buggy.

## Loaded

Lead a prison break and take on the might of the megalomaniac F.U.B. in this all-action 32-bit classic, now available on Evercade!

## Re-Loaded

The ex-convicts are back, and they brought friends! Blast your way through a series of explosive missions and take down the nefarious C.H.E.B. in this 32-bit sequel for Evercade.

## Hogs of War

Cry 'havoc!' and let slip the Hogs of War! Take on a series of missions or battle up to three friends in this hilarious 32-bit action-strategy game, now on Evercade.

## Buggy

Get ready for some chaotic 32-bit racing action in Buggy, now on Evercade. Compete against a field of opponents or explore the courses to find hidden secrets!
